Frequently asked questions

What is KP Prashna?

Prashna is the horary branch of Vedic astrology: instead of your birth chart, the astrologer casts a chart for the exact moment your question is asked. KP (Krishnamurti Paddhati) Prashna refines this with a precise sub-lord system and a number from 1 to 249 chosen by the querent, giving sharp yes/no answers.

Do I need my birth date, time or place?

No. That is the beauty of Prashna — the chart is cast for the moment you ask the question, so no birth details are needed at all.

Is Astroman AI free?

Yes — currently completely free. Open the bot, ask one sincere question, and get your reading.
